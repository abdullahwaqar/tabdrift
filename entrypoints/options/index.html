Search engine

Used when you search from the overlay. Put g, b or d before or after a search to use Google, Bing or DuckDuckGo just this once, like g esp32 pinout or esp32 pinout d.

Show your tabs, most recent first, as soon as the overlay opens. Press Ctrl+D on a tab to close it without leaving the overlay.

Show one history row per site instead of every page. Enter opens the site's front page, so it can log you in or redirect you. Shift+Enter opens the last page you visited there. Press the right arrow on a row to list its pages, and again to copy its domain or clean link.

Applies to history results and the open actions under Utilities. Off: Enter opens the link and closes the overlay, and Ctrl+Enter opens it in a background tab and keeps the overlay open, so you can open several results from one search. On: the two swap. On a Mac, use Cmd instead of Ctrl. Switching to an open tab always closes the overlay.

Paste an email or link into the overlay and its main result (the domain, or the clean link) is copied straight away.

Copy clean link

Copies the page's link without tracking parameters. Also in the right-click menu. Change the key under Manage Extension Shortcuts in about:addons.
