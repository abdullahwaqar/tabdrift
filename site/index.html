Search your open tabs without touching the mouse.

Tabdrift is a small Firefox extension that opens a floating search over whatever page you're on. Press the shortcut, type a few letters, hit enter, you're on the right tab. Nothing open matches? It searches your history and reopens the page in a new tab instead. Paste an email or a link and it hands you the domain or a tracker-free URL.

Alt+Shift+K to open
↑↓ navigate  •  Enter switch Tabdrift

How it works

No background tab-scanning. History is only searched once you've typed something that doesn't match an open tab.

01

Press the shortcut

The overlay is only injected into the page when you open it, so the extension isn't running on every tab all the time.

02

Type to filter

Matches against tab titles and URLs as you type, across every window, not just the current one. Matching is fuzzy, so a typo or two still finds the right tab.

03

No match? Check history

If nothing open fits, Tabdrift searches your history and lists close matches underneath, marked "history". Pick one and it opens in a new tab.

04

Jump

Arrow keys move the selection, enter switches to it and focuses that window. Esc closes without doing anything.

Emails and links

Paste an email or a link into the overlay and a Utilities section shows up above your tabs. The top row is already selected, so enter copies it.

Email to domain

Get the domain from an email address. It handles Name <a@b.com>, mailto: links and pasted lists (unique domains, one per line). The root domain is worked out for endings like co.uk and co.ae.

Sara Ahmed <sara@mail.acme.co.uk>
mail.acme.co.uk
acme.co.uk (root domain)

Links without the tracking

Strips utm_*, fbclid, gclid, msclkid and similar click ids, unwraps redirect links from Google, Outlook Safe Links, Facebook and LinkedIn, and shortens Amazon product links. Parameters that aren't tracking are left exactly as they were.

example.com/post?utm_source=news&id=7&fbclid=zzz
example.com/post?id=7

Copy without opening anything

Copy the current page's clean link with a shortcut, or from the right-click menu on any link. With Quick copy on, pasting into the overlay copies the result straight away.

Alt+Shift+C copy this page's clean link
Right-click a link, then Copy clean link

A pasted link also gets a Markdown link, a version without query and hash, its domain and root domain, an open-in-new-tab row and a Wayback Machine lookup. Everything runs locally and nothing leaves your browser. Copying uses the clipboardWrite permission, and the right-click item uses contextMenus.

Make it yours

Pick an accent color and where the overlay sits on screen, and switch Quick copy on or off, right from the same settings panel.
