Search your open tabs without touching the mouse.

Tabdrift is a small Firefox extension that opens a floating search over whatever page you're on. Press the shortcut, type a few letters, hit enter, you're on the right tab.

Alt+Shift+K to open
↑↓ navigate  •  Enter switch Tabdrift
Tabdrift in the middle of a search

How it works

No background tab-scanning, no page-content access until you actually ask for it.

01

Press the shortcut

The overlay is only injected into the page when you open it, so the extension isn't running on every tab all the time.

02

Type to filter

Matches against tab titles and URLs as you type, across every window, not just the current one.

03

Jump

Arrow keys move the selection, enter switches to it and focuses that window. Esc closes without doing anything.
