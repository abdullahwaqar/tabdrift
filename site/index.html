Switch tabs, search history and the web from one box.

Tabdrift is a free Firefox extension that opens a floating search over whatever page you're on. Press the shortcut, type a few letters, hit enter, you're on the right tab. Not open? It finds the page in your history. Not in history either? Enter searches Google, Bing or DuckDuckGo. Paste an email or a link and it hands you the domain or a tracker-free URL.

How it works

Nothing runs in the background. Tabdrift reads your tabs and history only while the box is open, and everything stays in your browser.

01

Press the shortcut

The overlay is only injected into the page when you open it, so the extension isn't running on every tab all the time.

02

Type to filter

Matches against tab titles and URLs as you type, across every window, not just the current one. Matching is fuzzy, so a typo or two still finds the right tab.

03

Not open? Check history, then the web

Close matches from your history show up under your tabs, one row per site. If nothing fits at all, the top row is a web search, so Enter takes you to the results.

04

Jump

Arrow keys move the selection, enter switches to it and focuses that window. Esc closes without doing anything.

Search the web from the same box

Tabdrift stands in for the address bar. Open it, type or paste, press Enter. No Ctrl+T and Ctrl+L first, and no typing @google to change engines.

One letter picks the engine

Put g, b or d before or after a search for Google, Bing or DuckDuckGo. That row jumps to the top, so Enter runs it. Plain searches use your default.

g esp32 pinout Google
esp32 pinout d DuckDuckGo
rust iced tutorial your default

Type an address, go

Full links, bare domains, local dev servers and IP addresses all get an open row. File names like README.md are left alone, so tab search still finds them.

localhost:5173

Open it where you want

A new tab by default, the tab you're on with Shift, or a background tab with Ctrl so you can queue up a few searches.

Your tabs still come first. When something you typed matches an open tab, the tab stays on top and the search sits under it, so switching works the way it always has. Searching for something like "vitamin d"? The key row goes first, and the full text with your default engine is one arrow press below. Searches go straight to the engine you picked. Tabdrift doesn't see, log or store them, and it needs no extra permissions for this.

Works on the new tab page too

Firefox doesn't let extensions draw on its own pages: the new tab page, other about: pages and addons.mozilla.org. There, the same shortcut opens Tabdrift from the toolbar instead, with the same search, keys and settings.

Replaces the empty tab

On a new tab, Enter loads the result right there instead of opening yet another tab. Shift+Enter opens a new one if you want it.

Pages it didn't expect

A few pages block extensions without saying so, like the built-in PDF viewer. The button flashes "!" the first time, and the next press opens Tabdrift from the toolbar.

Tip: pin Tabdrift to the toolbar so it opens right under its button.

History, one row per site

Dashboards and admin panels fill your history with long links full of IDs and session details. Tabdrift groups history results by site, so you get one row with a page count instead of a wall of URLs.

One row per site

Every page on the same site folds into a single row that shows the site, how many pages matched and the title of the latest one.

Expand it, copy from it

Right arrow lists the pages under a site, most recent first. Right arrow again on a page swaps the list for its copy options. Left arrow goes back.

→ list the pages, then copy options
← collapse, or go back

The pages listed under a site are the ones that match what you typed. The copy options are the same ones a pasted link gets: clean link, site link with no path, domain, root domain and a Markdown link. Prefer the plain list? Turn off Group history by site in settings. History is searched locally and nothing leaves your browser.

Or flip it

Want the overlay to stay open by default? Turn on Keep overlay open after opening a link in settings and the two keys swap.

Enter open in the background, stay
Ctrl+Enter open and close

It works on history rows and on the open rows under Utilities (open website, open clean link, Wayback Machine), and Ctrl-clicking a row does the same. Add Shift to open a site's last page instead of its front page. On a Mac, use Cmd instead of Ctrl. Switching to an open tab always closes the overlay, because you're leaving that page.

Emails and links

Paste an email and a Utilities section shows up with the domain already selected, so Enter copies it. Paste a link and the top row opens it, with the clean link and the other copy options right under it.

Email to domain

Get the domain from an email address. It handles Name <a@b.com>, mailto: links and pasted lists (unique domains, one per line). The root domain is worked out for endings like co.uk and co.ae.

Sara Ahmed <sara@mail.acme.co.uk>
mail.acme.co.uk
acme.co.uk (root domain)

Links without the tracking

Strips utm_*, fbclid, gclid, msclkid and similar click ids, unwraps redirect links from Google, Outlook Safe Links, Facebook and LinkedIn, and shortens Amazon product links. Parameters that aren't tracking are left exactly as they were.

example.com/post?utm_source=news&id=7&fbclid=zzz
example.com/post?id=7

Copy without opening anything

Copy the current page's clean link with a shortcut, or from the right-click menu on any link. With Quick copy on, pasting into the overlay copies the result straight away.

Alt+Shift+C copy this page's clean link
Right-click a link, then Copy clean link

A pasted link also gets a Markdown link, a version without query and hash, a site link with no path, its domain and root domain, an open-in-new-tab row and a Wayback Machine lookup. Everything runs locally and nothing leaves your browser. Copying uses the clipboardWrite permission, and the right-click item uses contextMenus.

Clean up your tabs

Open Tabdrift and your tabs are listed, most recent first. Close the ones you're done with without leaving the keyboard or waiting on each one to load.

Close as you go

Press the keys on the highlighted tab and it closes. The overlay stays open and the highlight moves down, so you can tap through a pile. Holding the keys doesn't repeat.

Ctrl+D close the highlighted tab

Duplicates in one go

When the same page is open more than once, a Clean up row appears above the list. One Enter closes the extras and keeps a copy of each page. Tracking parameters and #fragments are ignored.

Pinned tabs are never closed in bulk, and the tab you're on is never closed from its own overlay. Tabs closed from Tabdrift are ordinary closed tabs, so Ctrl+Shift+T (Cmd+Shift+T on Mac) brings them back. You can turn off the tab list on open in settings.

Common questions about Tabdrift

Is Tabdrift free?

Yes. Tabdrift is free and open source under the MIT license. There are no accounts, no ads and no paid tier. The source is on GitHub.

Can Tabdrift replace the Firefox address bar?

For most things, yes. Type a search and press Enter to search Google, Bing or DuckDuckGo, or type an address like github.com to open it. Put g, b or d before or after a search to pick the engine. Your open tabs and history show up in the same list, so one box covers switching, reopening and searching.

Does it work on the new tab page?

Yes. Firefox doesn't let extensions draw on the new tab page or other about: pages, so there the same shortcut opens Tabdrift from the toolbar instead. On an empty tab, Enter loads the result in that tab, so it behaves like the address bar.

Which search engines does it support?

Google, Bing and DuckDuckGo. Pick your default in settings. The keys g, b and d choose one for a single search, typed either before or after the search, like "g esp32 pinout" or "esp32 pinout d".

Does Tabdrift send my tabs, history or searches anywhere?

No. Tab and history matching happens inside your browser and nothing is sent to a server. Web searches go straight from Firefox to the engine you picked, the same as searching from the address bar. Tabdrift doesn't log them.

What's the keyboard shortcut?

Alt+Shift+K by default. You can change it in Tabdrift's settings. Alt+Shift+C copies the current page's link without tracking parameters.

Does Tabdrift work in Chrome, Edge or Safari?

No. Tabdrift is built for Firefox 109 and later, on Windows, macOS and Linux.

How do I close lots of tabs quickly?

Open Tabdrift and press Ctrl+D on a highlighted tab to close it. The highlight moves to the next one. A Clean up row closes duplicate tabs in one go, and typing a site name offers to close every matching tab. Closed tabs come back with Ctrl+Shift+T.
